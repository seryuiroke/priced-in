01

research question

how do leading ai and technology companies differ in growth, volatility, and long-term investment performance?

this project investigates whether higher investment returns consistently come with greater market risk. by comparing historical stock performance across major technology companies, the analysis explores how volatility, drawdowns, and long-term returns influence investment outcomes.

05

methodology

how the analysis was built

01

data collection

historical stock market data for eight leading technology companies was collected covering the 2020–2025 period.

02

data processing

python and pandas were used to clean, organize, and calculate cumulative returns, volatility, and maximum drawdown.

03

risk analysis

companies were compared using long-term return, annualized volatility, and maximum drawdown to evaluate investment performance.

04

visualization

matplotlib generated publication-quality figures while plotly created interactive versions for deeper exploration.

limitations

historical stock performance does not guarantee future returns. this analysis examines only eight companies and does not account for dividends, inflation, taxes, transaction costs, or portfolio rebalancing. differences in company age and market history may also influence comparisons.

06

key findings

major insights

  • NVIDIA generated the strongest cumulative return across the study period.
  • Microsoft demonstrated the lowest overall investment volatility.
  • Palantir experienced the largest historical maximum drawdown.
  • Higher long-term returns generally coincided with higher investment risk.
